The existing evidence does not establish those claims. Failed model/critic replacements remain inactive; no neural component is reintroduced. Missing free odds and inaccessible provider updates are not fabricated. Current manager cohorts and hypothetical account populations retain their sampling limitations.

The retained 575 weekly-team score and older continuous-account results have different protocols. No new historical points gain is claimed for 0.14. The new unlimited-search **full policy** has not acquired a protected continuous-account performance acceptance merely because its engineering regressions pass. Repeatedly studied historical seasons are not a newly untouched test set. Always-on hosting is outside this local application's runtime.

HiGHS completion establishes the additive objective within numerical tolerances; it does not establish the optimum of exact nonlinear autosubs or actual future football.
