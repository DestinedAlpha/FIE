# Football models, integration and scope · 0.6

The reference points forecaster and the structural football simulator are separate models. Their disagreement is reported. A lower conditional event error does not automatically establish a better complete transfer policy.

- `availability.py` learns transitions from dated official injured/doubtful reports. Its rotation model is tested against freshly refitted earlier-only production minute forecasts over all 16 two-match minute states. Serving conditions a second match within the same gameweek on simulated earlier minutes; it does not extrapolate that correction across ordinary gameweeks. Actual first-match minutes are labels only in the joint score.
- `discipline.py` distinguishes competition-specific accumulated cautions from cross-competition red-card service. Thresholds use club match ordinals. Published ban windows supersede earlier lists, and explicit decisions support appeals, stays, rescissions and cup fixtures. Unknown offence lengths and conflicting sources remain unresolved rather than becoming invented bans.
- `shot_data.py`, `attack.py` and `enrichment.py` join shot/role records conservatively by identity, club, fixture date and role. Open-play, set-piece and penalty opportunities remain separate. Credited scorers share the simulated club's actual goal total. A Poisson-binomial model learns FPL assist attribution by goal type, including own goals; no individual historical assist label is fabricated.
- `score_process.py` compares regularized team strength, independent Poisson, Dixon–Coles low-score correction, shared Gamma–Poisson tempo and shared Laplace coefficient draws. Earlier deadlines fit, a later calibration block chooses parameters, and subsequent deadlines assess proper scores. Unsupported complexity stays disabled.
- `odds.py` supports dated exhaustive 1X2/total markets, power de-vigging, goal-intensity constraints, paired scorer yes/no conversion and lead-time-specific reliability gates. Captures after an FPL deadline cannot qualify as pre-deadline calibration data. A free feed without Premier League markets leaves the statistical fallback active.
- `temporal_validation.py` separately tests recency decay and finishing shrinkage. Goals minus xG includes randomness, skill and measurement error. It is not an identified luck penalty. Existing neural/tree models retain the feature parameters stored in their training lineage; a new experiment cannot silently change their serving transform.

The temporal fusion model consumes histories, official news/set-piece duties, lagged qualified-manager choices, ownership/market data, other-competition workload and enriched shot/role/card features. Shared historical/live input builders and masks preserve missingness. The manager choice model receives genuine separate tree/neural forecasts and its branch's own earlier actions. Accepted behavioral policies enter rival comparisons; they do not become authoritative future manager transfers.

Source-supported specialist reports enter explicit numerical sensitivity analyses. Ambiguous training language does not prove availability, minutes or a starting place. Uncalibrated opinions do not receive a hidden points multiplier. Reddit supplies discovery leads; blocked access is not bypassed and raw Reddit posts are not a training corpus.

The weekly replay uses archived pre-deadline prices, registration and fixture schedules, plus earlier completed match facts and finalized historical labels imported later. Each team is frozen before target-week scoring. This is a historical reconstruction, not evidence that those decisions were actually issued before their historical deadlines.

Coverage and actual adoption decisions are in the generated evaluation artifacts.
